Airscape
In many industrialized cities, air is constantly present yet rarely perceived until pollution crises occur. AirScape addresses this sensory disconnection by designing with air and particles, translating seasonal and spatial airflow patterns alongside pollutants into embodied perception. By rendering air perceptually and physically present, the project invites reflection on the intimate yet neglected relationship between the atmosphere and the human body.
